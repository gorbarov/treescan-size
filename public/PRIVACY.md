# Privacy Policy

TreeScan Size does not collect, store or transmit any personal data.

- The app makes no network connections: no analytics, no crash reporting, no update checks, no accounts.
- Scan results (the list of your files and folders) stay in the app's memory on your Mac and are discarded when you quit.
- The app remembers only the path of the last folder you opened and your view settings (Size / On disk, a dismissed hint), in the standard macOS preferences on your Mac.
- The app changes files only when you ask it to: "Move to Trash" (after a confirmation) and, for Dropbox folders, "Don't sync" (sets the `com.apple.fileprovider.ignore#P` extended attribute that Dropbox uses).

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/gorbarov/treescan-size/issues.
